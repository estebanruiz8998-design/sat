FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this for the Digital SAT?

Yes. Everything — question formats, adaptive module structure, timing, and the eight skill domains — is modeled on the current Digital SAT.

How does the adaptive practice work?

Every question has a difficulty level and a skill tag. Answer correctly and the next question gets harder; miss, and it eases off while your tutor explains the concept. Your per-skill mastery score decides what you see next.

How accurate is the score prediction?

Your prediction is computed from your mock exam performance, weighted by question difficulty, and refined as you complete more practice. The more you do, the tighter the estimate.

Where is my data stored?

In this demo, all of your progress — diagnostics, plans, streaks, mastery — is stored locally in your browser. Nothing leaves your device. You can reset it anytime from Settings.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app, take the diagnostic, and start climbing. Your browser remembers your progress.
