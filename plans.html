Is the Free plan actually the full product?

In this demo, yes — diagnostics, plans, adaptive practice, modules, the tutor, analytics, and gamification all run entirely in your browser at no cost.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Paid plans are month-to-month with no lock-in; yearly plans are refundable pro-rata in the first 30 days.

What's the score guarantee?

On Elite, if you follow your plan (80%+ task completion) and don't improve at least 100 points from your diagnostic, you get your money back.
